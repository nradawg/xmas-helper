Xmas Helper

For moms whose kids moved out and stopped telling you what they want.

You paste one set of instructions into ChatGPT. It asks you about each kid, then writes a short message you text to them. They paste it into their own ChatGPT, it figures out what they actually want (sizes too), and they text the list back.

You paste their lists in and get exactly what to buy, where to get it near you, and about what it costs, all inside your budget.

How to use it

  1. Tap the green button below to copy the instructions.
  2. Open ChatGPT (Gemini or Claude work too), start a new chat, paste, and hit send.
  3. Answer its questions. Type, or tap the mic and just talk.
  4. It gives you a message for each kid. Text it to them.
  5. When they text their lists back, paste them into that same chat. Don't delete it.

Good to know

Your budget, what you already bought, and your own ideas stay out of your kids' messages. Still skim each one before you send it.

Lost the chat? Start a new one, paste the instructions again, and paste your kids' lists right under them.

Free ChatGPT works. If your AI can search the web, you get real prices and stores. Prices and stock change, so check the store's app before you drive over.

First names and a city or zip code are all it needs. No sign up, nothing to install.

Xmas Helper is free. Share it with another mom who needs it.
